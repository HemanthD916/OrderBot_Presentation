🔴 The Problem: Our Current Order Process Is a Bottleneck

Every new hardware order follows a fragile, manual path:

📧 Email Reception Manual
↓
📄 Read PDF Manual
↓
🔍 Check Salesforce Manual
↓
📊 Check Google Sheets Manual
↓
🧠 Decision Point Human
↓
✅ OK → Two manual emails
❌ Problem → Email sales rep (delay)

⚠️ Critical Pain Points

  • Delay: 2‑4 hours per order, often next day
  • Errors: Mis‑typed emails, miss‑checked inventory
  • Staff drain: 15+ hours/week wasted on copy‑paste
  • No visibility: No one knows order status until an email is sent

*Current state: ~20 orders/day, 1.5 full‑time roles absorbed by manual triage.

🔻 This is the biggest blocker in our hardware fulfillment pipeline.

🤖 The Solution: Introducing 'OrderBot'

An autonomous AI agent that handles the entire order intake process in seconds.

📧 Detect new order email Tool: Email API
↓
📄 Parse PDF & extract data Tool: PDF Parser
↓
🔍 Check customer status (Salesforce) Tool: Salesforce API
↓
📊 Check inventory (Google Sheets) Tool: Sheets API
↓
🧠 AI Decision Autonomous
↓
✅ All clear → Auto‑send two emails
⚠️ Issue → Flag for human, notify sales rep

✨ How It Works (Plain English)

  • Goal: Process every order within 5 minutes, 99.5% accuracy
  • Eyes & Ears: It monitors the inbox, reads PDFs, and talks to Salesforce & Sheets.
  • Brain: It checks rules (account active? stock >0?) and decides instantly.
  • Hands: It sends the correct emails and updates records automatically.
  • Safety Net: If anything is unclear, it pauses and alerts the ops team.

OrderBot works 24/7, never forgets an attachment, and never skips a check.
