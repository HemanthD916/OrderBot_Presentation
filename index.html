The Problem: Our Current Order Process Is a Bottleneck

Our current hardware order workflow relies heavily on manual human intervention, creating significant friction in our operations.

Key Pain Points:

  • Manual Data Entry: Staff waste hours reading PDFs and typing data into systems.
  • Costly Delays: Bottlenecks occur when staff are unavailable or overwhelmed with volume.
  • High Error Risk: Swapping between Email, Salesforce, and Sheets invites human error.
  • Poor Exception Handling: Order issues bounce back to sales reps, stalling the entire fulfillment lifecycle.

The Solution: Introducing 'OrderBot'

We propose OrderBot, an autonomous AI agent designed specifically to handle our end-to-end order workflow.

How it works:

  • Perception: Continuously monitors the inbox and uses AI to extract structured data from PDF attachments.
  • Action (API Integration): Autonomously queries Salesforce (account status) and Google Sheets (inventory).
  • Planning & Decision: Evaluates the data against our business rules instantly.
  • Resolution: Automatically routes fulfillment emails or flags exceptions to the team—without manual data entry.

Recommended Next Step: Read-Only Proof of Concept (PoC)

To validate OrderBot securely, we propose a 2-week "read-only" trial. OrderBot will process incoming emails and log its intended actions in a dashboard without sending emails or writing to live databases. This allows management to verify its accuracy and logic with zero risk to actual customer data before full deployment.
